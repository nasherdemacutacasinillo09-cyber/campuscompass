    Requirements

    What schools commonly ask for. Tick the ones you already have. Details differ per school.

    Documents

      Before the exam

      • Bring your exam permit, a valid ID, a ballpen, and a pencil. Check the calculator policy.
      • Try the route to the campus before exam day.
      • Leave early. Rush hour is roughly 6 to 9 AM and 5 to 8 PM.
      • Get a Beep card for the LRT and MRT.
      • Know your way home, and bring water and an umbrella.

      Some courses may also ask for

      • A portfolio for arts and architecture.
      • A medical certificate for health courses.
      • An audition for music, theater, and sports.
      • A recommendation letter, essay, or interview.

      How to apply

      The usual admission steps. Details differ per school.

      1. Pick a school and courseUse the filters above.
      2. Prepare your documentsReport card, PSA birth certificate, good moral certificate, and ID photo.
      3. Apply onlineCreate an account on the school's admission portal and submit the form.
      4. Pay the fee and get your exam scheduleSave your permit or confirmation number.
      5. Take the examThen wait for the school's official announcement of results.

      About CampusCompass

      CampusCompass is a website designed to help Grade 12 students take the next step toward their future with confidence. It makes finding college admission information easier by gathering important details, such as available courses, admission requirements, application deadlines, and entrance exam schedules, in one convenient platform. Students can also explore different schools and save their favorites while deciding which college best fits their dreams. With CampusCompass, choosing a college becomes easier, less stressful, and more exciting as students discover the right path toward their future.
